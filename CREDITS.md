# Credits and asset licenses

*The Third Light* setting, character writing and dialogue, Chapter 1 signing recollection, terrain, water and weather effects, prison and vintage vehicles, pump-house interior, radio-house worktable, north-jetty family props, original grass fallback, and Blender-generated building meshes were created for this project. Tamsin, Oren, Elias, and Iris use the credited CC0 MakeHuman character assets below; prison occupants and ambient drivers are original procedural meshes. The default meadow uses the MIT-licensed WindSweptGrass code module, adapted to the island terrain and paths; its blades and distant meadow are generated by code. Tree positions, proportions, and leaf artwork were authored here; their branch and leaf-card geometry is generated by the MIT-licensed EZ-Tree library. The 3D-density clouds, lightning, shoreline surf, rain impacts, and wet-window effects use original code and generated geometry or shaders. The shader-driven falling rain is original game code informed by the MIT-licensed Procedural Weather — Three.js Skill's vertex animation approach. Surfaces use the credited CC0 materials below. No Grassworks proprietary code or assets are included.

The post-rescue Iris exchange, resident gestures, Chapter 1 signing-memory text and state rules, and source-specific 3D evidence props are original game work and use no external speech or prop assets. The archive files, prison ledger, weather reel, tower records, jetty soundings, and tunnel water mark use generated geometry and labels. The period diesel set, centrifugal pump, gauges, conduits, lamps, pooled water, and pipe drips in `src/pumpInterior.js` are original procedural geometry and animation; no third-party pump model, texture, recording, or additional license is used.

The cliff-fall camera and screen treatment, recovery rules, and fall/sea-impact sound synthesis are original game code. They use the existing island geometry and water renderer, with no additional third-party asset.

The lodge stove fire, covered gatehouse brazier, generated 3D noise field, raymarched flame and smoke shaders, glow, and three in-engine story scenes are original game code and writing in `src/fireAtmosphere.js` and `src/cutscenes.js`. They use existing island/building geometry and no downloaded fire, smoke, cinematic, or audio asset.

The Chapter 1 passenger ferry and North Inlet rescue launch, their authored paint colors, contoured hull bands and fittings, distant coaster/trawler/cutter, offshore swell effects, and the mooring-line stall are original procedural geometry, texture work, animation, and writing in `src/ferryVisual.js`, `src/seaLife.js`, and `src/arrivalVoyage.js`. Both working decks reuse the bundled Poly Haven Brown Planks 03 CC0 maps. Their hulls and wheelhouses reuse the bundled Poly Haven Rusty Metal 04 CC0 normal and roughness maps for subtle pitting and worn paint, while their narrow boot stripes also use that material's diffuse map. The older Blender rescue boat is no longer loaded or bundled. No downloaded ferry or ship model is distributed.

The coastal gulls, feral sheep, and rabbits in `src/fauna.js` use original procedural geometry, materials, and animation. Their feather markings, fleece clusters, faces, ears, and paws use no downloaded animal models or textures.

## Roads, vehicles, and engine sound

The wet gravel road mesh, puddles, drive behavior, car wheel and brake-lamp animation, lodge stores, and radio generator hut are original procedural work for this game. Their visible material maps reuse the Poly Haven CC0 surfaces listed above. The low car engine tone is synthesized at run time in `src/audio.js`; no third-party vehicle model, motor recording, or film audio is included.

The seabirds, sheep, rabbits, and seated ambient drivers are original low-poly procedural meshes. Ambient traffic uses the game's original vehicle geometry and road network. Bird calls and passing-engine tones are synthesized at run time in `src/audio.js`; no animal recording or additional third-party model is included.

The gate and wing guards and detainees are also original procedural figures built from shared Three.js geometry. Their short ambient lines were written for this game. Those prison figures use no third-party model, animation, or voice.

Nearby resident gaze and speaking gestures are original runtime animation. All four named residents retain procedural loading fallbacks; their detailed models use the CC0 assets credited below. No motion capture or recorded performance is used.

The four generated character GLBs use `EXT_texture_webp` with lossless WebP
maps. The offline repacker checks decoded RGBA pixels against the original
embedded PNGs. The final package stores repeated images once under
`public/assets/shared_images/`; the GLBs reference those same image bytes by
relative URI. All ten GLBs share 33 distinct image files, with provenance and
per-file hashes in `tools/glb_shared_images_manifest.json`. Geometry and
material metadata were verified unchanged by the deduplication step. The
combined GLB and image payload is 21,461,132 bytes smaller than the preceding
embedded-image files. A browser with WebP support is required. Source PNGs and
the offline CC0 pack remain outside the game package; reproduction steps are
in [`docs/character-candidate.md`](docs/character-candidate.md).
